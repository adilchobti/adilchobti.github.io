02 / METHODOLOGY

From assumptions
to risk measures.

01Data & dependencies

Historical returns are used to estimate expected returns, volatility and the correlation structure between assets.

02Scenario generation

Correlated random shocks are generated using Cholesky decomposition and Geometric Brownian Motion.

03Portfolio construction

Equal-weight, minimum-variance and maximum-Sharpe allocations are compared under a 30% weight constraint.

04Evaluation

VaR, CVaR, Sharpe ratio and maximum drawdown complement a walk-forward backtest with monthly rebalancing.

Backtest statistics are historical results for the selected period and asset universe; they are not indicative of future performance.
